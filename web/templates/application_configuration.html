Password policy

Lengths count Unicode characters. Bcrypt has a hard cap of 72 UTF-8 bytes. Some characters use up to four bytes; choose a maximum comfortably below 72. Passwords over the byte cap are rejected, never truncated. Existing passwords remain valid.

Scan configuration

Amass and DNSX still require a Full Mode profile. Enable DNSX or directory discovery only after selecting a matching wordlist.

Select one or more lists. Disabling a stage preserves its selection.

Upload wordlists

UTF-8 text only, up to 1 GiB per file, 10 GiB total, 50 lists. Lines may contain up to 4 KiB. Blank lines and # comments are ignored during scanning. Uploads do not automatically enable scanning.

    Tools Settings

    The shared queue accepts up to 100 waiting scans. Lowering concurrency lets active scans finish before starting more work. Directory discovery uses at most 50 simultaneous requests per scan and stops after two hours, retaining partial findings.
